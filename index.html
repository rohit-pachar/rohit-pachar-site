Nights, weekends, and the simple joy of making something. A side project with no meetings to book and no permission to ask.

Build on the Go

A change of scenery. Not of pace.

Travelling across India with founder friends, building along the way. Good company, fresh perspectives, and work that doesn’t need a fixed address.

The idea behind the detour

Get a group of builders together. Take the conversation somewhere new. Keep making things as you go. BOG brings the road trip and the building session into the same itinerary.

03 / A LITTLE BACKSTORY

Small-town roots.
Wide-open possibilities.

From a village near Sikar
to a few worlds I hadn’t imagined.

I grew up in Bhairunpura, Rajasthan. My route to building things ran through chemical engineering at IIT Bombay, a design minor at IDC, and a lot of late nights making things with friends.

The subjects changed. The instinct didn’t: understand how something works, then try making something of my own.
